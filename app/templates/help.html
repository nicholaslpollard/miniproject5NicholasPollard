Help

This page explains how the Personal Task Tracker will be used once its features are complete.

Account and task features are still in development. The steps below describe the intended workflow.

Getting Started

  1. Create an account with a username and password.
  2. Log in to reach your personal task list.
  3. Add tasks with a title, description, and due date.
  4. Update tasks by editing them or marking them complete.
  5. Log out when you are finished to end your session.
